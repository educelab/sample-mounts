# `villa.2026-10` vs upstream

How the `villa.2026-10` style compares with
[ScrollPrize/villa's scrollcase](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/tree/main/foundation/scrollcase)
at commit `7d6a82c` (2026-10-01). Upstream was run in its own environment
with its pinned dependencies (build123d `30bbe96`, meshlib 3.0.5.215).
Comparisons done 2026-10-01.

## Case bodies: exact match

Upstream's `build_case` and our `left_body`/`right_body` were built for
four scroll sizes (radius × height in mm): 38 × 155, 20 × 60,
44.41 × 167.58, and 32.83 × 178.06. Upstream puts Z=0 at the scroll bottom,
so our bodies were shifted down by `scroll_z`.

For all eight bodies (left and right at each size), the volumes and
bounding boxes match, and the symmetric difference between ours and
upstream's is 0.000 mm³. This includes the caps, bolt tabs, nut pockets,
mounting holes, arrow, top-cap text, curved divider with end posts, and
kinematic disc with its 0.5 mm clearance.

`tests/test_villa_reference.py` keeps it that way, using reference volumes
and bounding boxes generated by `scrollcase/scripts/villa_reference.py`.

## End to end: PHercParis3 and PHercParis4

Both pipelines were run on `phercparis3-scaled.ply` and
`phercparis4-scaled.ply`: upstream's `build_lining`, `build_case`, and
`combine_brep_case_lining`, and our `scrollcase build` with
`style = "villa.2026-10"`.

| | Fitted radius | Fitted height | Holes (L + R) | Separate bodies (L / R) | Min. scroll clearance | Blocked slide-out paths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PHercParis3, upstream | 44.60 | 166.55 | 26 | 41 / 13 | 1.80 mm | 0 of 300 |
| PHercParis3, ours | 44.41 | 167.58 | 0 | 1 / 1 | 1.95 mm | 0 of 300 |
| PHercParis4, upstream | 32.93 | 178.05 | 2 | 1 / 1 | 1.82 mm | 0 of 300 |
| PHercParis4, ours | 32.83 | 178.06 | 0 | 1 / 1 | 1.94 mm | 0 of 300 |

- **Fit.** Our tighter fit tolerance gives a 0.1–0.2 mm smaller radius.
  Heights agree within 1 mm.
- **Mesh quality.** Upstream's PHercParis3 halves have open holes and many
  separate fragments; ours are closed single bodies.
- **Clearance.** The target is `lining_offset` = 2 mm. Upstream's coarser
  voxels (0.4% of the mesh diagonal, about 0.8 mm here) leave 1.8 mm; ours
  (0.4 mm) leave 1.95 mm.
- **Placement.** Our scrolls sit 180° about the case axis from upstream's.
  Both pipelines pick the wide axis's sign arbitrarily, and because the S
  is point-symmetric, a 180° turn swaps which part of the scroll goes in
  which half. Turned 180°, upstream's PHercParis4 scroll is within 0.45 mm
  (mean) of ours. PHercParis3 is also turned 6° by upstream's wide-axis
  calculation, which doesn't normalize its vector before `arccos`; turned
  180°, it's within 1.2 mm. Total case volume agrees within 0.1%; only the
  left/right split differs. For these two scrolls,
  `scroll.rotate = [0, 0, 180]` reproduces upstream's orientation.
